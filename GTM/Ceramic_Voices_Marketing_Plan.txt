Ecommerce Marketing Plan
for Ceramic Voices
A comprehensive strategy for growing an artisan ceramics ecommerce business
Prepared by CMO Growth Guide
Executive Summary
Ceramic Voices is an artisan ceramics ecommerce business featuring handcrafted pieces from featured artists
like Ryan (Ran Xiangfei) and Liu Kun. The brand positioning centers on "Where Artisans Speak Through Color"
with a focus on storytelling, craftsmanship, and bringing color and hope into homes. Products range from
decorative vases to functional drinkware (tea sets, mugs), with price points from $72.99 to $200+.
Current Strengths:
• Strong brand voice and compelling artisan stories
• Free worldwide shipping over $120
• Established Shopify infrastructure
• Multiple payment options
• Current holiday sale (up to 50% off)
Key Opportunities:
The artisan/handmade ceramics market attracts buyers seeking unique, meaningful pieces over
mass-produced items. This positions Ceramic Voices well for gift-giving occasions, home decor enthusiasts,
and collectors.
Target Audience Profiles
Primary Audience: The Intentional Home Curator
Adults 30-55 who invest thoughtfully in their living spaces. They prefer quality over quantity, appreciate knowing
the story behind what they buy, and see home decor as an expression of personal values. Household income
$75K+. They shop for themselves and for meaningful gifts.
Secondary Audience: The Gift Giver
People searching for unique, memorable gifts for weddings, housewarmings, holidays, and milestone
occasions. They want something special that shows thought and care, not another generic gift card or
mass-produced item.
Tertiary Audience: Ceramic Collectors and Enthusiasts
People who specifically collect handmade ceramics, follow ceramic artists, and appreciate the craft. They
attend pottery shows, follow ceramics hashtags, and are willing to pay premium prices for exceptional pieces.
Marketing Strategy by Channel
1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and AI Search Visibility
On-Page Optimization
Product descriptions should be expanded significantly. Each product page needs:
• The artisan's name and studio prominently featured
• The story behind the piece or collection
• Materials and dimensions
• Care instructions
• Keywords naturally woven throughout (handmade ceramics, artisan pottery, porcelain vase, ceramic
mug, etc.)
Content Strategy for Organic Traffic
Create a blog with articles targeting search intent:
• "How to Style Colorful Ceramics in a Neutral Room"
• "The Art of Chinese Porcelain: A Modern Interpretation"
• "Caring for Handmade Ceramics: A Complete Guide"
• "Choosing the Perfect Ceramic Gift for Any Occasion"
• "What Makes Handmade Ceramics Worth the Investment"
Technical SEO
• Ensure all images have descriptive alt text
• Add schema markup for products (price, availability, reviews)
• Optimize page load speed (critical for image-heavy sites)
• Submit sitemap to Google Search Console
2. Email Marketing
List Building
Add a prominent email signup with a compelling offer:
• "Join our list for first access to new artisan collections + 10% off your first order"
• Pop-up after 30 seconds on site or on exit intent
• Footer signup on every page
Welcome Sequence (5 emails over 2 weeks)
1. Welcome + discount code + brand story
2. Meet the artisans (Ryan and Liu Kun profiles)
3. How our pieces are made (behind the scenes)
4. Customer favorites and reviews
5. Reminder: your discount expires soon
Abandoned Cart Sequence (3 emails)
1. Reminder at 1 hour: "You left something beautiful behind"
2. At 24 hours: Highlight the artisan who made that piece
3. At 72 hours: Final reminder, possibly with small incentive
Post-Purchase Sequence
• Order confirmation with care instructions
• Shipping notification
• Delivery follow-up with request for review
• 30 days later: Complementary product suggestions
• 60 days later: Invite to follow on social/join community
Ongoing Campaigns
• New collection announcements
• Artisan spotlight stories
• Seasonal gift guides
• Sale notifications (sparingly, to maintain premium positioning)
• Behind-the-scenes content from the artisans' studios
3. Paid Advertising
Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads
Top of Funnel: Awareness
• Video ads showing the creation process (clay to finished piece)
• Carousel ads featuring the artisan + their work
• Target: Interior design interests, home decor enthusiasts, handmade/artisan interests, Etsy shoppers,
pottery/ceramics interests
• Lookalike audiences based on past purchasers
Middle of Funnel: Consideration
• Retarget website visitors with product-specific ads
• Dynamic product ads showing items they viewed
• Customer testimonial ads
• "Why handmade matters" educational content
Bottom of Funnel: Conversion
• Retarget cart abandoners with urgency messaging
• Limited-time offers for warm audiences
• Free shipping threshold reminders ("You're $X away from free worldwide shipping")
Budget Recommendation: Start with $1,500-2,500/month to test and optimize.
• 40% to prospecting/awareness
• 30% to retargeting
• 30% to conversion campaigns
Pinterest Ads
Pinterest is highly relevant for home decor purchases:
• Shopping ads linked to product catalog
• Idea pins showing styling inspiration
• Target home decor, interior design, gift shopping keywords
Google Ads
Search Campaigns:
• Brand terms: "Ceramic Voices"
• Product terms: "handmade porcelain vase," "artisan ceramic mug," "colorful ceramic tea set"
• Gift terms: "unique housewarming gift," "handmade wedding gift"
Shopping Campaigns:
• Feed optimized with detailed product titles
• High-quality images
• Competitive pricing displayed
4. Social Media Strategy
Instagram (Primary Platform)
Content Pillars:
• Product beauty shots (40%): Styled photos of pieces in beautiful settings
• Process content (25%): The artisans at work, close-ups of techniques, kiln openings
• Artisan stories (20%): Personal content about Ryan and Liu Kun, their philosophies, their studios
• Customer content (15%): Repost customer photos, unboxing videos, reviews
Posting Cadence: 4-5 feed posts per week, daily stories
Reels Strategy:
• Quick process videos (15-30 seconds)
• Before/after reveals
• Packing orders
• Sound trending audio when appropriate
Hashtag Strategy: Mix of broad reach (#ceramics #homedecor #handmade) and niche (#ceramicart
#potterylove #studioceramics #contemporaryceramics)
Pinterest
• Create boards for each collection and use case (Vases, Tea Sets, Gift Ideas, etc.)
• Pin every product multiple times with different images and descriptions
• Create lifestyle pins showing pieces in room settings
• Post 10-15 pins per day (mix of original and repins)
TikTok
Focus on process content that performs well on this platform:
• Satisfying pottery videos
• Artisan day-in-the-life content
• Packing orders ASMR style
• The "story behind this piece" format
5. Influencer and Partnership Marketing
Micro-Influencer Strategy
Partner with:
• Home decor/interior design accounts (10K-100K followers)
• Lifestyle bloggers who feature their homes
• Tea ceremony and matcha enthusiasts (for the drinkware collection)
• Gift guide creators around holidays
Compensation Options:
• Product seeding (gift pieces in exchange for posts)
• Affiliate commission (10-15% of sales they drive)
• Flat fee for larger creators
PR and Editorial
Pitch to:
• Design publications (Architectural Digest, Dwell, Domino)
• Gift guides in lifestyle publications
• Local publications in the artisans' home regions
• Ceramic arts publications and blogs
6. Conversion Rate Optimization
Website Improvements to Prioritize
• Add customer reviews to product pages (use Loox, Judge.me, or similar)
• Expand the "About Us" and artisan pages with richer storytelling and video
• Add a size guide with common objects for reference
• Show the free shipping threshold prominently in cart and on product pages
• Add urgency elements during sales (countdown timers, "only X left")
• Consider adding Klarna/Afterpay for payment flexibility on higher-priced items
• Add a gift wrapping option
Trust Builders
• Display shipping and returns policy prominently
• Add FAQ page addressing common concerns
• Show secure payment badges
• Feature customer testimonials on homepage
