# Abu Dhabi Couple's Photography & Food Itinerary (Feb 18-20, 2026)

A romantic 3-day getaway focusing on stunning visuals, culinary delights, and relaxation.

## 🗓️ Itinerary Overview

### Day 1: Feb 18 (Wednesday) - Architectural Marvels & Cultural Immersion
*   **Morning (09:00 AM)**: **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque**.
    *   *Photography Tip*: Arrive early for soft light and fewer crowds. Capture reflections in the pools.
    *   *Dress Code*: Modest dress required.
*   **Lunch**: **Meylas** (Authentic Emirati Cuisine) or **Al Fanar**.
*   **Afternoon**:
    *   **Wahat Al Karama**: Viewpoint opposite the Mosque for panoramic shots.
    *   **Qasr Al Watan** (Presidential Palace): Opulent interiors and golden hour architecture.
*   **Evening**:
    *   **Dinner**: **Sontaya** (St. Regis Saadiyat). Southeast Asian cuisine on floating pavilions.
        *   *Vibe*: Extremely romantic, sunset views.
        *   *Must-Try*: Sunset Dim Sum, Curries.

### Day 2: Feb 19 (Thursday) - Art, Nature & Island Bliss
*   **Morning**: **Louvre Abu Dhabi**.
    *   *Photography Tip*: The "Rain of Light" under the dome is best in the morning or late afternoon.
    *   *Activity*: Kayaking tour around the museum for unique angles.
*   **Lunch**: **Fouquet's Abu Dhabi** (Louvre) or **Beirut Sur Mer** (Mamsha Al Saadiyat).
*   **Afternoon**: Check-in to **St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort**.
    *   Relax at the pristine white sand beach.
*   **Late Afternoon**: **Jubail Mangrove Park** or **Desert Safari**.
    *   *Option A (Relaxed)*: Sunset boardwalk stroll in Jubail Mangroves.
    *   *Option B (Adventure)*: Private Sunset Desert Safari with BBQ Dinner. (Great for golden hour dune photos).
*   **Dinner**: **Buddha-Bar Beach** (St. Regis).
    *   *Vibe*: Chic, sophisticated, beachfront. Pan-Asian/Nikkei cuisine.

### Day 3: Feb 20 (Friday) - History, Shopping & Departure
*   **Morning**: **Qasr Al Hosn**
    *   *Photography Tip*: Stark white watchtower against modern skyline.
    *   *Activity*: Explore the oldest stone building in Abu Dhabi and the House of Artisans.
*   **Lunch**: **Erth** (Modern Emirati) at Al Hosn site or **The Galleria Al Maryah Island**.
*   **Afternoon**:
    *   **Option A (Shopping & Views)**: **The Galleria Al Maryah Island**. Luxury shopping and great promenade views.
    *   **Option B (Theme Park)**: **SeaWorld Abu Dhabi** or **Ferrari World** (Yas Island). SeaWorld is great for indoor cool and visuals (Endless Ocean realm).
*   **Late Afternoon**: Return to St. Regis, freshen up/late check-out if arranged.
*   **Dinner**: Early dinner at **Mazi** (Greek) at St. Regis or **Cipriani** (Yas Marina) for a final view of the F1 track/marina.
*   **Evening (08:30 PM)**: Transfer to Airport.
*   **Departure**: 11:00 PM Flight.

## 📸 Top Photography Spots
1.  **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque**: Reflective pools, white marble columns.
2.  **Louvre Abu Dhabi**: Light rain effect under the dome, waterfront exterior.
3.  **Liwa Desert / Al Wathba**: Golden sand dunes at sunset.
4.  **Observation Deck at 300**: Panoramic city skyline views.
5.  **Sontaya Terrace**: Floating pavilions at sunset.

## 🍽️ Dining Highlights (St. Regis & More)
*   **Sontaya**: Romantic, floating pavilions, Thai/Asian.
*   **Buddha-Bar Beach**: Trendy, sunset beats, Pacific Rim cuisine.
*   **Meylas**: Local Emirati hospitality and flavors.
